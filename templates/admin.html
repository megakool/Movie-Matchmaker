Connection Types

Cast
The same actor appears in all 4 movies.
e.g. Movies starring Tom Hanks
Director
All 4 movies were directed by the same person.
e.g. Films directed by Christopher Nolan
Award
All 4 movies are connected by a shared award win or nomination.
e.g. Best Picture Oscar winners
Genre
All 4 movies share the same genre, tone, or style.
e.g. '80s slasher films
Setting
All 4 movies take place in the same location, time period, or world.
e.g. Movies set in ancient Rome
Plot
All 4 movies share a core plot device or story element.
e.g. Movies where the protagonist loses their memory
Trope
All 4 movies share a well-known cinematic or narrative trope.
e.g. Movies with an unreliable narrator
Franchise
All 4 movies are part of the same series or cinematic universe.
e.g. Marvel Cinematic Universe films
Cast (Special)
A cast member plays the same recurring character type across the 4 movies — not the same character, but the same archetype or role.
e.g. Movies where the same actor always plays the villain
Cast (Meta)
The 4 movies are linked by a real-life fact about a shared cast member — something outside the films themselves.
e.g. Movies featuring actors who later became directors
